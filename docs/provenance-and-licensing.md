# Provenance and licensing

Provenance makes research auditable without redistributing sensitive source material. Persist normalized provider kind, timestamp, a known vendor-field label when permitted, and a content hash. Keep citations to public sources or user-authorized opaque references; do not store credentials, account identifiers, client IPs, raw positions, sensitive paths, full vendor payloads, or unrestricted source text.

A content hash proves which input was used but does not grant a right to retain or share it. Before adding a source, document ownership, license/entitlement, allowed users, geographic and purpose restrictions, retention, redistribution, attribution, derived-data, and deletion requirements. Keep raw licensed data in its authorized system and use synthetic fixtures in tests. Reports must distinguish provider evidence, analyst inference, missing data, and reviewer limitations.

When terms are unclear, disable persistence/export for that source and obtain approval from the data owner or counsel. This project documentation is operational guidance, not legal advice.
